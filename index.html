A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?
We’re here to help ↗

Do I have to connect my bank?+

No. You can use Apple Pay automations, bank SMS alerts, imported statements, receipt photos, or manual entries. Link a supported bank when it works for you.

Can I use it without a budget?+

Yes. Tracking mode gives you a categorized record of your spending without setting any limits. You can start a budget whenever you’re ready.

What if a transaction shows up twice?+

BudgetGo flags potential duplicates from different sources. Review the match, then merge the entries or keep both. You stay in control.

How do I set up automatic tracking?+

Use Apple Shortcuts to send Apple Pay payments or bank alerts to BudgetGo. Our step-by-step setup guides walk you through it.
