Good questions.
Clear answers.

Need help? Contact support.

Does BudgetGo make payments for me?

No. BudgetGo tracks spending. You still pay with your usual cards, wallets, or banking apps.

Do I need to connect my bank?

No. You can add purchases yourself, import transaction files, or set up Apple Shortcuts automations. Supported bank connections are another option when they’re available for your account.

Can I use BudgetGo on my phone?

BudgetGo is available for iPhone. See the App Store listing for current device and iOS requirements.

How much does BudgetGo cost?

An active subscription is required after the free trial. You’ll see current pricing and trial terms in the app before subscribing. Prices can vary by country or region.

How do I set up automatic tracking?

Use Apple Shortcuts to send supported Apple Pay transactions or bank messages to BudgetGo. Choose the cards or messages you want to track, then review the imported spending in the app. Our step-by-step guides walk you through setup.

What control do I have over my data?

You can review, edit, delete, and export your transaction records. We don’t sell your personal information. Our privacy policy explains what we collect, how it’s used, and your choices.
