Need help? Contact support.

Can I track spending without setting a budget?

Yes. Use BudgetGo to track and understand your spending on its own. When you want a spending limit, set one active budget and allocate it across your categories.

How does my spending get into BudgetGo?

Link a supported bank through Plaid, use Apple Shortcuts for Apple Pay or bank SMS alerts, import a file or receipt photo, or add a purchase yourself. You can combine the sources that fit your day. Our setup guides cover the Shortcuts options.

Will my bank work with BudgetGo?

Search for your bank when linking an account in BudgetGo. Connections use Plaid, and support depends on your institution, country, and account. If yours isn’t listed, you can still import statements or track purchases yourself.

Can I change the categories AI chooses?

Yes. AI helps sort your transactions, and you can review or change any category. Your records stay yours to adjust.

Can I bring in my old transactions?

Import a PDF bank statement, CSV or Excel export, or receipt photo. Review the transactions before confirming the import. Files can contain up to 500 transactions and be up to 5 MB.

Can I see upcoming bills and subscriptions?

Yes. BudgetGo detects recurring bills and subscriptions and shows upcoming charges together, so you can plan around them. It tracks the payments; you manage or cancel subscriptions with their providers.

How much does BudgetGo cost?

BudgetGo offers a free trial followed by a subscription. Check the app for the current plans, pricing, and trial terms before subscribing.

Can I export or delete my spending records?

Yes. You can edit or delete transaction records and export them as CSV. We don’t sell your personal information. Read our privacy policy for details about how your data is used.
