How Much Does Thanksgiving Dinner Cost? (Budget Per Person, With the Math)

Key takeaways

  • A basic home-cooked Thanksgiving dinner costs far less than most people spend. The extras (drinks, dessert, hosting) are what push the bill up.
  • Set a budget per person, add a separate line for extras, then keep a 10% buffer.
  • Shop in two trips at most and log every receipt, so you can see the total before Thursday.

How much does Thanksgiving dinner cost? It depends on how many people you feed and what you count. This guide gives you a realistic Thanksgiving dinner budget per person, a worked example for 10 guests, and a simple way to keep the total under control.

How much does Thanksgiving dinner cost in 2026?

There's no single official 2026 number yet, so treat any figure as a planning range. For a baseline, the American Farm Bureau Federation's 2025 survey of a basic, traditional dinner for 10 came to about $5.52 per person. Looking ahead to 2026, a MoneyLion guide on Yahoo Finance suggests planning for $8 to $10 per person, or about $80 to $100 for 10 guests. That range is the guide's own estimate, not a measured average.

The gap between $5.52 and $8 to $10 is useful. The survey prices a simple menu with no extras. A real dinner usually has more dishes, drinks and a dessert.

Step 1: Count the people, then set a per-person number

Start with the head count, including kids. Then pick a per-person number you're comfortable with. If you cook the classics from scratch, somewhere in the planning range above is a fair target. If you're hosting a bigger menu, go higher, and decide that before you shop, not at the checkout.

Step 2: Add a separate line for everything else

The turkey is rarely the expensive part. These extras are where hosting budgets slip:

  • Drinks: wine, sparkling water, cider.
  • Dessert: bought pies often cost more than you expect.
  • Hosting items: foil pans, napkins, candles, takeaway containers for leftovers.
  • Last-minute top-ups: the forgotten butter or the extra bag of ice.

Keeping these in their own line shows you what the extras really cost. It also pairs well with how you already budget groceries. Our guide to setting a grocery budget shows how to build the monthly number this sits inside.

A worked example (with the math)

Say you're hosting 10 people and you plan $9 per person for food, the middle of the planning range.

Example Thanksgiving dinner budget for 10 guests
LineHow it worksTotal
Food10 people × $9$90.00
DrinksWine and sparkling water$40.00
DessertTwo pies$25.00
Subtotal$90 + $40 + $25$155.00
Buffer10% of $155$15.50
Total budget$155 + $15.50$170.50

That's $17.05 per person all in. Food alone is $9 per person, which shows how drinks and dessert change the picture. If $170.50 is more than you want to spend, trim the extras first, not the main meal.

How to cut the cost of Thanksgiving dinner

  • Ask guests to bring a dish. A potluck can cut your food bill sharply and guests usually like helping.
  • Cook fewer sides. Three well-made sides beat seven average ones.
  • Buy store brands for staples. Stock, flour, butter and canned goods are hard to tell apart in a finished dish.
  • Check what you already have. Spices, oil and baking basics are the usual forgotten duplicates.
  • Plan the leftovers. Decide on two meals from the turkey and the extra shopping disappears.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Thanksgiving dinner cost per person?

A basic traditional dinner for 10 cost about $5.52 per person in the American Farm Bureau's 2025 survey. For 2026, one planning guide suggests $8 to $10 per person. Your own number depends on your menu, drinks and dessert.

How much should I budget for a Thanksgiving dinner for 10?

Multiply your per-person food number by 10, add a line for drinks, dessert and hosting items, then keep a 10% buffer. In the example above, that comes to $170.50.

Is it cheaper to cook or buy a prepared meal?

Cooking from scratch is usually cheaper per person, especially for a larger group. A prepared meal saves time, so compare the total price with your own budget before you decide.

How do I stay on budget when I'm hosting?

Set the total before you shop, ask guests to bring a dish, shop in one or two trips and log every receipt. Checking the running total mid-week lets you adjust while there's still time.
