Last updated October 22, 2026
This Privacy Notice for GraphLeaf LLC ('we', 'us', or 'our'), describes how and why we might access, collect, store, use, and/or share ('process') your personal information when you use our services ('Services'), including when you:

Questions or requests: Email support@budgetgo.ai. The sections below explain processing, retention and applicable privacy rights.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information you provide and connect

  • Account and support information: Your email address, name where provided, authentication information, preferences and messages or attachments you send to support.
  • Financial records: Amounts, currencies, dates, merchants, account details and balances, categories, budget allocations, recurring payments, tags and other information you enter or import. You may use one active budget or track without a budget.
  • Files and images: Statements, spreadsheets, receipt photos and their extracted content when you use import features. File processing can take place on our servers and with processing providers.
  • Connected sources: Account, balance and transaction information from banks linked through Plaid and supported Apple Wallet accounts through FinanceKit, within the access you authorize. Shortcuts automations send the Wallet payment details or selected bank alerts you configure.
  • Voice entry: Audio, transcripts, draft transactions and relevant context such as currency, timezone and category names. Voice processing can send audio and text to our servers and AI providers. Optional on-device captions do not mean the entire voice feature runs locally. Review voice drafts before saving.
  • Purchases: App-store purchase information, subscription status and identifiers used to manage your access through RevenueCat. The store handles payment-card details.

Financial information and device permissions

Financial and account-login information may be sensitive under applicable law. Use optional connections and imports only for information you are authorized to provide. Microphone, photo, notification and financial-data permissions are requested for the relevant features. You can manage supported permissions in the app or device settings.

Bank SMS alerts passed through Shortcuts are parsed on your device. Only recognized financial alerts create transactions. Extracted details may be saved and synced, but the raw message is not saved as the transaction description or sent through the transaction sync queue. This is different from sending us a statement or using voice entry.

Biometric unlock uses your device's authentication service; BudgetGo does not receive your fingerprint or Face ID template. Motion used for tilt privacy and hiding amounts is processed on your device.

Technical, usage and diagnostic information

We and our providers process device and app information, IP addresses, timestamps, service logs, feature interactions and errors. An IP address may indicate an approximate location; this is different from continuous GPS tracking.

PostHog receives usage events and account identifiers, including your email and name where supplied. Events cover activity such as onboarding, imports, bank connections, transaction actions and insights. Current import analytics exclude filenames, file contents, audio, transcripts and account details. These analytics restrictions do not describe the data needed to process an import or AI request.

When enabled, Sentry error and performance reporting can include an account identifier, diagnostics, screenshots, view hierarchy and error-triggered session replays. Replay text and images are configured to be masked. Masking and credential filters do not guarantee that all personal or financial information is removed from every diagnostic record.

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process information to create and authenticate accounts; store and sync financial records; import and extract transactions; reconcile sources and identify possible duplicates; categorize spending; and generate analytics, forecasts, recurring-payment predictions and personalized insights.

We also process information to manage subscription access, deliver enabled notifications, respond to support and privacy requests, understand feature usage, diagnose failures, protect the Services and meet legal obligations. If you join the MCP waitlist, we use your email to send related updates; you can ask us to stop those messages.

The legal bases applicable to processing are addressed in section 3. An operating-system permission or acceptance of our Terms does not replace consent where data protection law requires separate consent.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We disclose information to providers as needed for the features you use and service operation:

  • Accounts, storage and sync: Supabase supports authentication, server-side records and file storage.
  • Connected accounts and Apple features: Plaid supports authorized bank connections; Apple supports Wallet, FinanceKit and Shortcuts. Provider authorization and privacy terms also apply.
  • AI and document processing: OpenAI, OpenRouter and the model providers handling requests process relevant inputs and outputs. OpenRouter routes requests to the model providers processing them. E2B provides temporary environments for document parsing and processing. Some receipt-processing paths also use Amazon Web Services document analysis.
  • Usage and diagnostics: PostHog provides product analytics. Sentry provides error, performance and replay diagnostics. Langfuse supports AI tracing and usage monitoring; traces may contain request context, inputs and outputs depending on the feature and tracing configuration.
  • Purchases: Apple or the applicable store processes purchases, and RevenueCat manages subscription information and access.
  • Sign-in and communications: Apple or Google provides account information when you choose its sign-in option. Email and notification delivery providers process the information needed to deliver messages you receive.

We may also disclose information where required by law, to protect rights and service security, or in connection with a merger, financing or transfer of our business, subject to applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. Providing data to service providers to operate BudgetGo is distinct from selling it or sharing it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

International processing

Our providers may process information outside your country. The locations and transfer arrangements depend on the provider and service involved. Contact support@budgetgo.ai for information about the arrangements applicable to your data. Accepting our Terms or continuing to use BudgetGo is not blanket explicit consent to international transfers.

5. DO WE OFFER ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE-BASED PRODUCTS?

AI supports transaction extraction from files and voice, categorization, reconciliation assistance, spending insights and forecasts. Relevant inputs, outputs and context are processed by our service and AI providers, including OpenAI and requests routed through OpenRouter.

Depending on the feature, this can include statement or receipt content, audio, transcripts, transaction details, category names, budget information and records retrieved to answer a request. AI tracing may also process request context and outputs.

Credential and sensitive-data filters help reduce exposure in some processing and diagnostic paths. They do not make every input anonymous or guarantee removal of all personal or financial information.

AI output may be inaccurate or incomplete. You can review and change your records and categories. Choosing not to use voice or file imports avoids those optional inputs, but it does not disable every AI feature. Contact support@budgetgo.ai about AI-processing choices and applicable privacy rights.

6. HOW DO WE HANDLE YOUR SOCIAL LOGINS?

If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifiers and profile information supplied for authentication, which may include your email address and name. Apple may provide a private relay email address. The information depends on your provider and choices.

We use that information to authenticate you and manage your BudgetGo account. Your sign-in provider's privacy notice governs its own processing. A sign-in connection does not authorize access to your contacts or social-network posts.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain account information and saved financial records as needed to provide the Services while your account is active, subject to your deletion requests and applicable legal requirements. Deleting a source file does not delete transactions saved from it.

Uploaded import files expire 24 hours after their import batch is created and are scheduled for automated deletion. Failed cleanup attempts are retried, so expiry does not guarantee that every copy is erased at exactly 24 hours. Saved transactions, import metadata, diagnostic records and provider-held data have separate retention lifecycles.

Account deletion is available in Settings. The app requests server-side account deletion and, after a successful response, removes user-owned records and connection credentials stored on that device and signs out. For help, or to exercise applicable access, correction or deletion rights, contact support@budgetgo.ai.

Some records may be retained where necessary for legal obligations, security, billing or resolving disputes. Backup copies may remain until their retention cycle ends, and third-party providers may retain records under their applicable obligations and terms. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup, provider or device. Stopping an import or revoking access does not automatically erase previously collected records.

11. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ('DNT') feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.
California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognising or honouring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
